Metroid-Inspired Level Design

The Metroid Inspired Level is a Metroidvania-style level built around the key elements that define the genre. Players explore a nonlinear world, uncovering gated areas locked until the right key or powerup is obtained. RPG-like progression rewards exploration, while design encouragements and built-in level clues guide players forward without holding their hand. Every corner of the level is designed to make players feel the satisfaction of earning their way through the world.

Project Details:

• Solo Project
• Role: Level Designer
• Built in Unity Web Player - Metroid Level Editor
• 3 Week Development

Responsibilities

  • Created and developed a Metroidvania-style level built around gaining new abilities to unlock new rooms and areas.
  • Made rough sketches and early layout concepts to visualize world structure, room connections, and progression flow.
  • Implemented world exploration with teasers to guide players, including hidden items and environmental clues that hint at where to go next.
  • Designed a progression system where specific items are required to access certain areas, such as needing the High Jump Boots to reach the Varia Suit.
  • Conducted playtesting sessions to observe player behavior and iterate based on feedback.

The Design Process

Pre-Production

Having never played the original Metroid before, the first step was to playtest and get familiar with the core movement, mechanics, powerups, and enemy behavior. To do this, a small prototype level was built as a way to explore and understand how the game worked firsthand. This hands-on approach helped brainstorm ideas and build a clearer picture of how the level could be structured and progressed before moving into full production.

Design & Execution

Phase 1: The Cave and Learning the Basics

As stated in the macro chart, the main goal of Phase 1 is to ensure players can control their character and get comfortable with the core mechanics. This section focuses on introducing traversal, climbing, mantling, and stealth — giving players the space to learn and practice before any real challenge is introduced.
